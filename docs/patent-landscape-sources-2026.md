## Official Indian patent guidance

**IP India — Guidelines for Examination of Computer Related Inventions (CRIs),
2025.** IP India's current guidelines page lists the CRI 2025 guidance and
illustrative annexures. The guidance discusses Section 3(k), including the
exclusion of mathematical/business methods, computer programmes per se and
algorithms, and says substance rather than claim form is examined.

## WIPO patent-search standards

WIPO PCT Guidelines Chapter 11 states that prior art generally includes
everything made available to the public anywhere in the world by any means
before the relevant date, including internet disclosures.

WIPO Chapter 12 explains that novelty is defeated when every element or step is
explicitly or inherently disclosed in prior art.

WIPO Chapter 13 explains that inventive step is assessed for the claimed
invention as a whole and that multiple items of prior art may be combined when
assessing obviousness.

## Closely related patent disclosures

### US20260073260A1
"System and method for causality-augmented generative intelligence to discover
non-obvious insights from heterogeneous data sources."

Priority shown in Google Patents: 2025-11-17. The disclosure combines
heterogeneous/multimodal ingestion, causal representation, causally constrained
generative reasoning and counterfactual validation.

URL:
https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260073260A1/en

Differentiation target: FinAI should not claim the broad architecture. The
working claim hypothesis instead requires a concrete prefix-level causal
reachable/necessary certificate and action masking before expensive structural
simulation.

### CN121146937A
"A method and apparatus for financial stress testing based on generative
artificial intelligence."

Priority: 2025-11-17; publication: 2025-12-16. The claims cover heterogeneous
financial data, a weighted directed asset graph, hierarchical Bayesian
parameterization, a conditional graph-generative network, Monte Carlo
simulation and risk reporting.

URL:
https://patents.google.com/patent/CN121146937A/en

Differentiation target: do not rely on generic generative financial stress
testing; focus on the computational screening mechanism.

### US20260057252A1
"Task variable causal graph construction method and apparatus, device and
medium."

Priority shown: 2024-08-20. The disclosure uses LLM priors, data independence
tests and Monte Carlo tree search to construct causal graphs for downstream
tasks.

URL:
https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260057252A1/en

Differentiation target: candidate stress-action feasibility against a terminal
risk interval, rather than generic causal graph construction/search.

### CN121146043A
"An intelligent information decision-making system based on reinforcement
learning."

The disclosure uses dynamic causal modeling, policy-generated candidate actions,
counterfactual inference and reward/policy updates.

URL:
https://patents.google.com/patent/CN121146043A/en

Differentiation target: the candidate action itself is tested against explicit
reachable/necessary intervals before expensive structural evaluation.

### WO2025067134A1
"Generative flow model training method and apparatus, and action prediction
method and apparatus."

Priority shown: 2023-09-28; publication: 2025-04-03. The disclosure describes
GFlowNet-style sequential state/action generation on graph-structured processes.

URL:
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025067134A1/en

Differentiation target: GFlowNet is an implementation embodiment, not the
claimed inventive center.

## Non-patent literature

### Financial causal stress testing
Gao, Mishra & Ramazzotti (2017), "Efficient Simulation of Financial Stress
Testing Scenarios with Suppes-Bayes Causal Networks", Procedia Computer Science
108, 272–284, DOI 10.1016/j.procs.2017.05.167.

The paper explicitly targets financial stress scenarios using probabilistic
causal networks and reports reduced computational complexity relative to
conventional Monte Carlo approaches.

Source:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050917307500

### Causal data science for financial stress testing
Gao, Mishra & Ramazzotti (2018), "Causal data science for financial stress
testing", Journal of Computational Science 26, 294–304.

The paper describes financial stress testing with causal networks and reports
reducing computation by classifying branches as profitable or risky.

Source:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877750317311377

## Practical patent conclusion

The search surface is crowded across:
- causal financial stress testing;
- financial generative stress testing;
- causal-graph search and pruning;
- sequential generative action selection;
- GFlowNet training;
- counterfactual evaluation.

Accordingly, the FinAI technical hypothesis should remain narrow: an
intervention-aware, finite-state/linear-DAG feasibility certificate combines
forward reachable intervals and backward necessary intervals with pre-expensive
structural evaluation action masking, descendant-cone incremental recomputation,
and measured compute/recall telemetry.

No novelty, inventive-step, enforceability or freedom-to-operate conclusion is
made here. A filing-grade opinion still requires a professional patent search
covering patent families, non-patent literature, prosecution histories, and the
relevant jurisdiction.
