# FinAI Research Gap, Novelty Hypothesis, and Competitive Landscape

## Purpose

This document defines the research contribution FinAI should test rather than
claim in advance. The goal is to distinguish implemented engineering from
empirically demonstrated scientific novelty.

## Current research gap

Recent financial AI work already covers many individual ingredients:

- CAMEF combines time series and macroeconomic announcement text with causal
  learning and LLM-generated counterfactual augmentation.
  https://doi.org/10.1145/3711896.3736872
- FSTGAT combines strict temporal causality with dynamic graph attention for
  non-stationary financial systems.
  https://doi.org/10.3390/sym17081344
- GRID applies GFlowNet trajectory generation to financial tail-risk discovery
  using a learned risk oracle.
  https://github.com/anony-sub-papers/GRID
- CausalGNN-Fin (May 2026 preprint) describes heterogeneous financial graphs,
  cross-modal transformer alignment, causal message passing, and temporal risk
  evolution.
  https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.35656.69123

Therefore FinAI must not claim novelty from simply combining the names
GFlowNet, TGAT, multimodal learning, or causal graphs.

### Proposed gap

A stronger gap is the absence of a clearly validated closed-loop system in
which:

1. point-in-time multimodal evidence forms a financial state;
2. causal candidates are discovered and filtered by temporal stability;
3. the graph is conditioned on regime information;
4. a generative model searches for diverse stress trajectories under causal
   and financial constraints;
5. every generated trajectory is propagated through the same structural model;
6. statistical plausibility, causal consistency, tail-risk behavior and
   uncertainty are evaluated jointly;
7. the entire loop is tested with strict future-only, ablation and
   out-of-distribution evaluation.

This is a research hypothesis, not a prior-art guarantee.

## FinAI proposed contribution

### Causal-Regime-Constrained Financial Stress Generation

The central hypothesis is that stress trajectories generated under explicit
causal-graph, regime, empirical-support and portfolio constraints can be more
useful and more internally consistent than trajectories generated by an
unconstrained scenario generator.

The proposed loop is:

Market/Macro/News/Filings/Events/Entities/Portfolio
-> Multimodal state
-> temporal causal discovery
-> stability + consensus
-> regime-conditioned graph encoder
-> causal empirical GFlowNet
-> structural intervention propagation
-> tail metrics + uncertainty
-> symbolic validation
-> evidence-backed explanation

## What should be measured

### Prediction
- MAE
- RMSE
- directional accuracy where appropriate
- calibration error

### Tail risk
- VaR coverage
- CVaR/expected-shortfall error
- exceedance frequency
- tail quantile calibration

### Scenario quality
- historical-support distance
- causal-consistency rate
- regime-consistency rate
- scenario diversity
- duplicate/degenerate trajectory rate

### Graph quality
- edge stability
- sign consistency
- graph recovery on synthetic ground truth
- sensitivity to variable scaling
- sensitivity to omitted/confounded variables

### Robustness
- normal regime
- high-volatility regime
- crisis-like windows
- missing modalities
- shifted distributions
- noisy text/events
- perturbed causal graphs

## Required ablations

A final experiment should compare:

1. market-only model;
2. multimodal model without causal constraints;
3. causal model without GFlowNet;
4. GFlowNet without causal constraints;
5. regime-aware model without multimodal evidence;
6. full FinAI system.

The contribution is supported only if the full system produces measurable gains
on predefined metrics without relying on hindsight or future information.

## Competitive landscape

FinAI is not positioned as a replacement for proprietary financial-data
platforms. The differentiation target is the research mechanism.

| Platform/category | Documented 2026 capability | FinAI research differentiation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloomberg ASKB | Agentic market/company research over Bloomberg data with source attribution | Explicit causal stress trajectories and intervention loop |
| LSEG Workspace AI Search/Deep Research | Verifiable research across trusted market/news/filing content | Causal graph admission and scenario validation |
| FactSet Portfolio Analytics MCP | Governed portfolio analytics in conversational/agentic workflows | Generative systemic-stress analysis constrained by graph structure |
| AlphaSense | Multi-agent generative search, deep research, workflow agents and cited outputs | Mechanism-first scenario generation rather than primarily research retrieval |
| S&P Capital IQ Pro | Fundamental data, ChatIQ, Document Intelligence, Chart Explainer | Dynamic causal stress modeling |

| OpenBB | Open financial workspace and AI-native workflows | Explicit causal-regime stress-generation research loop |

## Non-claims

FinAI must not claim:

- first-ever causal + multimodal finance system;
- first-ever financial GFlowNet;
- first-ever temporal financial GNN;
- production foundation model;
- causal identification from NOTEARS alone;
- empirical validation without a point-in-time dataset and future holdout;
- superiority over Bloomberg, LSEG, FactSet, AlphaSense, or other platforms
  without a defined benchmark and measured results.

## Novelty evidence package required before publication

Before a paper or thesis claims novelty, create:

1. systematic literature matrix;
2. patent/prior-art search;
3. feature-by-feature competitor matrix;
4. reproducible benchmark dataset description;
5. ablation results;
6. failure analysis;
7. implementation commit and data/model artifact versions.

The phrase "novel" should be attached to a specific tested contribution, not to
the presence of advanced model names.
